## Source Baseline Status

**Status:** Frozen for the initial assessment, 26 October 2026.

The initial source baseline covers the Public Safety module requirements in §7.5, relevant cross-cutting technical and security provisions, Annex I data sources and indicators, and Annex II infrastructure requirements.

The source requirements register records the identified requirements and their references to the technical specification. The evidence-gaps register records questions that cannot be resolved from the reviewed specification alone.

This baseline establishes a documentary reference point for the assessment. It does not establish that specified components, integrations or controls have been implemented, tested or shown to operate effectively.

If further source material or material inconsistencies are identified, the baseline may be updated through a documented revision.
