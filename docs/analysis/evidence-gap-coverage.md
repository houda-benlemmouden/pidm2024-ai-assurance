# Evidence-Gap Coverage

This view shows which documented evidence gaps are linked to each identified risk in the risk register.

## Interactive heatmap

Teal cells indicate that the risk register records a relationship between a risk and an evidence gap. Grey cells indicate that no relationship is recorded.

## Interpretation and limitations

The matrix currently represents 11 identified risks, 8 evidence gaps, and the relationships recorded in the risk register.

A recorded relationship does not mean that an evidence gap has been resolved, that a control has been implemented, or that a risk has been eliminated. An unmarked cell means only that the relationship is not recorded in the register.

The chart is generated by `scripts/generate_evidence_gap_heatmap.py` from the existing risk register and evidence-gap document.
