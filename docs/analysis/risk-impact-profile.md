## Interpretation

The current register records:

- **8 risks** with Major impact (score 4).
- **3 risks** with Moderate impact (score 3).

These are inherent impact scores, not calculated overall risk ratings. Likelihood and residual-risk scores remain unassessed because the available evidence does not currently support them. Assessment priority and evidence confidence are separate fields.

## Method and limitations

The impact scale ranges from 1 (Negligible) to 5 (Severe). Impact should reflect the most serious reasonably foreseeable consequence supported by the risk scenario, rather than the worst imaginable outcome.

The chart is generated by `scripts/generate_risk_impact_profile.py` from the risk register. It does not establish that a risk has occurred, that a control is effective, or that the described system is operational.
